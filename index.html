Settings

Audio

Lossless quality

Remaster

A mastering chain on your own signal: rumble filter, corrective EQ, de-esser, compressor, limiter. It cannot add detail your microphone never heard, and what you send is no longer raw — the transport stays lossless, the source does not.

Microphone processing

Off keeps the signal untouched. Wear headphones, or the far end hears itself.

Screen share

Nothing here is clamped down for you. Your display and your uplink are the real limits: asking for 16K at 360 fps gets you whatever the source can actually produce, and a ceiling your connection cannot carry will stutter.

Camera

Whatever the webcam can actually produce, at whatever rate the link will carry. Most webcams top out well below these numbers, and the resolution you get back is the sensor's, not this setting's.
